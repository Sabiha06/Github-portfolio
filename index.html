Emergency Room Operations Dashboard

Tools: Tableau, SQL

Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze emergency room utilization, patient demographics, and operational performance using de-identified ER visit data. The dashboard was designed to support hospital administrators and operational stakeholders in monitoring ER demand, identifying peak congestion periods, and understanding patient mix.

What this dashboard answers:

  • Total ER visit volume and overall utilization trends
  • Peak days and hours of ER activity using a time-based heatmap
  • Patient distribution by age group and race
  • Average ER wait time and trends over time
  • Patient satisfaction trends
  • Department-level visit volumes to identify high-demand services

Business value:

  • Enables leadership to quickly identify peak congestion windows
  • Supports staffing and resource allocation discussions
  • Provides visibility into demographic and departmental demand patterns

Planned enhancements:

  • Breakdown of admission vs discharge outcomes
  • Wait time and satisfaction analysis by department and time of day
  • Incorporation of patient acuity (triage level) to contextualize wait times
